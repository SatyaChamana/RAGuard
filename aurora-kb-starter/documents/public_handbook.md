# Aurora Health Technologies — Employee Handbook (Public Excerpt)

## About this handbook
This handbook summarizes general policies for all Aurora Health Technologies employees. It is shared publicly on our careers site and may be distributed freely.

## Working at Aurora

**Remote work.** Aurora operates on a hybrid model. Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval. Teams set their own in-office anchor days to keep collaboration predictable.

**Working hours.** Standard hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM in the employee's local time zone, with flexibility for teams that coordinate across regions. Core overlap hours are 11:00 AM to 3:00 PM Central Time.

## Time off and leave

**Paid time off.** Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per year, accrued monthly, in addition to 10 company-observed holidays. PTO rolls over up to a maximum of 5 unused days into the following year.

**Parental leave.** Aurora offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave for all new parents, regardless of gender or path to parenthood.

**Sick leave.** Employees receive 8 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO.

## Benefits overview
Aurora provides medical, dental, and vision coverage starting on the first day of employment, a 401(k) with a 4% company match, an annual $1,500 professional development stipend, and a monthly $75 wellness reimbursement.

## Code of conduct
All employees are expected to act with integrity, protect patient and customer data, and follow Aurora's information security and privacy policies. Aurora handles protected health information on behalf of its customers and treats data protection as a core responsibility, not a checkbox.

## Equal opportunity
Aurora Health Technologies is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, national origin, age, disability, or veteran status.
