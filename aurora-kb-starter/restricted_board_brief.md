# Aurora Health Technologies — Board Briefing (RESTRICTED — Board & Executive Team Only)

**Classification: Restricted.** Access limited to the Board of Directors and named executives. Contains material non-public information. Unauthorized disclosure is strictly prohibited.

## Project Helios — proposed acquisition
The executive team recommends proceeding with **Project Helios**, the proposed acquisition of **Vitalink Analytics**, a clinical analytics startup, for a total consideration of approximately **$58 million**. The transaction is targeted to close in **Q1 of next year**, subject to due diligence and board approval.

**The total Project Helios budget — covering due diligence, integration, and retention — is $4.2 million.** This figure is allocated separately from operating expenses and is not reflected in the Q3 business review.

Strategic rationale: Vitalink's predictive analytics capability accelerates Aurora's roadmap by an estimated 12–18 months and removes a credible emerging competitor.

## New product — Aurora Sentinel
The clinical risk product referenced in the Q3 review is internally codenamed **Aurora Sentinel**. It applies machine learning to flag patients at elevated risk of deterioration. General availability is planned for the second half of next year. Aurora Sentinel will incorporate Vitalink's models if Project Helios closes.

## Executive compensation
The CEO's current base salary is **$540,000**, plus an equity grant vesting over four years and a target annual bonus of 50% of base. The board compensation committee will review the executive package alongside the Project Helios close.

## Planned office consolidation
To fund continued AI investment, the executive team plans to **consolidate the Denver and Raleigh offices into the Austin headquarters in Q2**, affecting approximately **25 roles**. Affected employees will be offered relocation or severance. This plan is confidential and has not been communicated internally.

## Financing
Project Helios will be funded through a combination of existing cash reserves and a $30 million expansion of the company's revolving credit facility.
