# Aurora Health Technologies — Company FAQ (Public)

## What does Aurora Health Technologies do?
Aurora Health Technologies builds software that makes clinical documentation effortless for care teams. Our mission is to give clinicians their time back so they can focus on patients instead of paperwork.

## When was Aurora founded?
Aurora Health Technologies was founded in 2016.

## Where is Aurora headquartered?
Aurora is headquartered in Austin, Texas, with additional offices in Denver, Colorado and Raleigh, North Carolina.

## How large is the company?
As of this year, Aurora employs approximately 480 people across its three offices and a distributed remote workforce.

## What products does Aurora offer?
Aurora offers two flagship products:
- **AuroraScribe** — an ambient clinical documentation assistant that listens to patient visits (with consent) and drafts structured clinical notes for the clinician to review.
- **AuroraConnect** — a secure patient messaging and engagement platform that integrates with electronic health record systems.

## Who are Aurora's customers?
Aurora serves hospitals, health systems, and outpatient clinics across the United States. Customers use Aurora to reduce clinician documentation burden and improve patient communication.

## Is Aurora hiring?
Yes. Aurora is growing, with a particular focus on engineering, machine learning, and customer success roles. Open positions are listed on the Aurora careers site.

## How does Aurora protect data?
Aurora is HIPAA-compliant and follows strict data protection practices. Patient and customer data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is governed by role-based controls.
