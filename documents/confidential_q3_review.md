# Aurora Health Technologies — Q3 Business Review (CONFIDENTIAL — Internal Only)

**Classification: Confidential.** For internal employee distribution only. Do not share externally.

## Financial summary
Aurora closed Q3 with **revenue of $32.6 million**, up 28% year over year. **Operating margin for the quarter was 18.4%**, an improvement from 14.1% in the same quarter last year, driven by improved gross margins on AuroraScribe and reduced cloud infrastructure costs.

Annual recurring revenue (ARR) reached **$128 million** at the end of Q3.

## Customer metrics
- **Active enterprise customers: 240** hospitals and clinics.
- **Net revenue retention: 114%**, reflecting strong expansion within existing accounts.
- **Annual logo churn: 6%**, slightly better than the 7% target.
- **Customer acquisition cost (CAC) payback: 14 months.**

AuroraScribe remains the primary growth driver, accounting for 71% of new bookings this quarter. AuroraConnect attach rate among new customers rose to 48%.

## Operational highlights
- The AI engineering team shipped a new retrieval pipeline for AuroraScribe that reduced note-generation latency by 35%.
- Support ticket volume per customer dropped 12% following the Q2 onboarding redesign.

## Q4 plan
- **Headcount: hire 60 additional employees in Q4**, with the majority allocated to AI engineering and machine learning roles.
- Expand the AuroraScribe specialty-coverage roadmap to cardiology and orthopedics.
- Begin early-access pilots for a new clinical risk product (details under separate restricted cover).

## Risks
- Cloud inference costs remain the largest variable expense; the team is evaluating on-premise and open-weight model hosting to control spend.
- Two enterprise renewals representing 4% of ARR are at moderate risk and under active management.
